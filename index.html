About Me

I am a Zambian environmental and development researcher with a background in geography, climate change, food systems, and sustainable development. My work brings together field research, quantitative and qualitative approaches, stakeholder engagement, and data-driven analysis to explore development and environmental challenges.

My research interests include food security, climate resilience, environmental sustainability, rural livelihoods, post-harvest losses, climate policy, carbon markets, natural resources, mining and local development, and data-driven approaches to policy research.
